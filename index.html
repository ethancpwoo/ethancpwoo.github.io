About Me:

Hi! My name is Ethan and I am currently studying my undergrad in mechatronics engineering at the University of Waterloo. I've been interested in robotics and autonomy for a while, and I have worked on multiple robotic personal projects. From my exposure in autonomy, I realized that I like to see my code work on a physical level, leading me to work more towards embedded systems and controls. I really would like to gain more experience in working with lower level code and understand the depths of this field.


As for now, I've been trying to work more at my undergraduate research position at the Control, Learning and Logic Group, where the group is trying to setup an autonomous vehicle stack on a Jeep Cherokee. I've also been working more on Watonomous and UWARG, where I am working on their HAL and embedded flight systems.


On a personal note, I've played baseball for most my childhood but haven't touched it since an elbow injury. I also played badminton from high school onwards and trained until university. Nowadays, I do play badminton recreationally, but I am looking to rejoin some softball or maybe some other sports.

Projects

Quadrupedal Simulation

I built this quadruped with my roommate. He worked primarily on the mechanical robot, I worked more on the autonomy. I used RL and PyTorch to train the quadruped on how to walk. I created a simulation of the robot using PyBullet and did the sim-to-real transfer using ROS2.



SLAM Spacemapper Robot

I used OpenCV and C++ to program a robot running an Monocular ORB-SLAM algorithm. Algorithm ran on a Raspberry Pi 3, using an Arducam. The robot was designed with OnShape and with a L298N motor controller driving the motors. ORB-SLAM created a map of the area the robot covered.
